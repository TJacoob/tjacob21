greatr is a fitness platform made for personal trainers in Portugal. Trainers manage their clients, build training plans by hand or generate them with AI, schedule sessions and follow each client's progress set by set, leaving coaching notes the client reads before the next workout. Clients follow their plan, log every set, book sessions and message their trainer, all in the same app.

I built the app's frontend using Nuxt, Tailwind and Pinia, packaged with Capacitor so a single codebase ships to iOS, Android and the web, working alongside a backend developer on the server-side and its AI features. I also built greatr.pro, the product's website, including its download pages and help center.
